How to Play:

  1. Roll the dice to get a 6 (Ship), 5 (Captain), and 4 (Crew)
  2. Click on dice to select which ones to keep
  3. Click "Keep Selected" to set aside chosen dice
  4. Roll remaining dice up to 3 times total
  5. Once you have Ship, Captain, and Crew, the remaining dice sum is your score
